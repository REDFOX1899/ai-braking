# AI Braking proposed product portfolio

Revision 0.1 | Concept only | 7 September 2026

These 12 workstreams describe a broad portfolio, not 12 finished products. Customer relevance is a hypothesis until supported by RFQs. The order favours shared engineering and test evidence; it is not a claim of market share. No torque or size ratings are assigned to AI Braking products yet.

## Sequencing rationale

Begin common inspection/monitoring while specifying both core mechanical platforms. Service parts and rotors develop with those platforms. Buy specialist actuation and friction components first. Expand into additional interfaces and proprietary subsystems after validation. Keep impact buffers and storm/locking systems in separate test programmes.

Each family below balances intended customer relevance, component reuse, supplier strategy and validation burden. Supplier availability and economics remain unverified until quotations arrive.

## AB-MON - Monitoring and inspection
Stage: Foundation | Maturity: concept

Intended applications: All selected industrial segments.

Reuse opportunity: Common data acquisition, calibration, event logs and inspection records.

Make/buy: Buy industrial sensors and DAQ; develop software and fixtures.

Required evidence: Measurement uncertainty, mounting repeatability, data quality, false alarms.

Do not put AI in the safety loop. Start advisory.

Competitor references: dellner-bubenzer, svendborg, emg. See the manufacturer collection reports for source URLs and gaps.

## AB-DR - DIN interface drum brakes
Stage: Core platform | Maturity: concept

Intended applications: Factory cranes, industrial winches and material handling.

Reuse opportunity: Base, levers, springs, shoes and adjustments across controlled size variants.

Make/buy: Design assembly; outsource metalwork; buy thruster and lining initially.

Required evidence: Holding/dynamic torque, release time, thermal duty, wear, fatigue.

Approve one real duty envelope before selecting the first sizes.

Competitor references: galvi, sibre, dellner-bubenzer. See the manufacturer collection reports for source URLs and gaps.

## AB-DC - Spring-applied disc calipers
Stage: Core platform | Maturity: concept

Intended applications: Conveyors, winches, cranes and heavy industry.

Reuse opportunity: Caliper architecture, lining retention, mounting and spring arrangements.

Make/buy: Design assembly; outsource bodies; buy hydraulic actuation parts.

Required evidence: Contact balance, torque, thermal growth, release clearance, seal/spring life.

Parallel requirements work; separate lead needed for parallel detailed design.

Competitor references: twiflex, stromag, svendborg, galvi, sibre. See the manufacturer collection reports for source URLs and gaps.

## AB-SP - Service parts and friction kits
Stage: Alongside core | Maturity: concept

Intended applications: Installed AI Braking product families.

Reuse opportunity: Shoes, pads, bushes, pins, seals and adjustment kits tied to revisions.

Make/buy: Buy characterized lining compound; outsource controlled parts.

Required evidence: Friction-pair validation, retention, lot consistency and fit.

Start with our own assemblies; competitor compatibility requires separate assessment.

Competitor references: galvi, mondel, svendborg. See the manufacturer collection reports for source URLs and gaps.

## AB-ROT - Drums, discs and hubs
Stage: Alongside core | Maturity: concept

Intended applications: Both brake platforms.

Reuse opportunity: Machined rotating interfaces and documented material variants.

Make/buy: Outsource casting/forging/machining to approved specifications.

Required evidence: Thermal cycles, surface finish, runout, balance, speed and hub strength.

Keep each friction pair and rotor material configuration identifiable.

Competitor references: galvi, mondel, twiflex, svendborg. See the manufacturer collection reports for source URLs and gaps.

## AB-AIST - AISE/AIST interface shoe brakes
Stage: Expansion | Maturity: concept

Intended applications: Steel mills, ports and export replacement projects.

Reuse opportunity: Reuse proven mechanisms where requirements permit; distinct interface families.

Make/buy: Design adaptations; outsource metalwork; buy actuation.

Required evidence: Imperial interfaces, severity of duty, endurance and machine qualification.

Not just a unit conversion from DIN.

Competitor references: galvi, mondel. See the manufacturer collection reports for source URLs and gaps.

## AB-ACT - Electrohydraulic thrusters
Stage: Expansion | Maturity: concept

Intended applications: Drum and thruster-operated disc brakes.

Reuse opportunity: Motor, pump, piston, seals and force/stroke variants.

Make/buy: Buy initially; evaluate proprietary design after performance and cost studies.

Required evidence: Force-stroke-time curves, temperature, orientation, leakage and endurance.

Treat as its own development programme, including electrical requirements.

Competitor references: emg, galvi, dellner-bubenzer. See the manufacturer collection reports for source URLs and gaps.

## AB-HPU - Hydraulic power units
Stage: Expansion | Maturity: concept

Intended applications: Hydraulically released calipers and emergency brakes.

Reuse opportunity: Reservoir, pump, valves, filtration, sensing and manifolds.

Make/buy: Integrate purchased qualified components before custom hydraulic development.

Required evidence: Release/application transients, contamination, leakage, loss of power and pressure.

Controlled braking requires system dynamics and machine interface requirements.

Competitor references: galvi, svendborg, stromag, dellner-bubenzer. See the manufacturer collection reports for source URLs and gaps.

## AB-COUP - Flexible and gear couplings
Stage: Expansion | Maturity: concept

Intended applications: Drive trains with brake drums/discs.

Reuse opportunity: Shared hub interfaces and modular rotating components.

Make/buy: Buy proven couplings initially; design only against defined demand.

Required evidence: Misalignment, torsion, balance, fatigue and lubrication.

Keep coupling torque capacity distinct from braking torque.

Competitor references: galvi, twiflex, dellner-bubenzer. See the manufacturer collection reports for source URLs and gaps.

## AB-POS - Positive-actuated brakes
Stage: Application-led | Maturity: concept

Intended applications: Process machinery and controlled braking applications.

Reuse opportunity: Reuse selected caliper or shoe elements with distinct actuation.

Make/buy: Buy actuator/control components; design mechanism variants.

Required evidence: Loss-of-actuation behaviour, modulation, timing and application integration.

Does not inherit spring-applied fail-safe behaviour.

Competitor references: galvi, twiflex, stromag. See the manufacturer collection reports for source URLs and gaps.

## AB-BUF - Industrial buffers
Stage: Separate programme | Maturity: concept

Intended applications: Cranes, travelling equipment and end stops.

Reuse opportunity: Energy absorber families selected by actual impact requirements.

Make/buy: Initially partner with specialist energy-absorber suppliers.

Required evidence: Impact energy, peak force, stroke, recovery, temperature and repeated impacts.

Cannot qualify from brake torque tests.

Competitor references: galvi, sibre. See the manufacturer collection reports for source URLs and gaps.

## AB-SPEC - Storm, locking and special systems
Stage: Separate programme | Maturity: concept

Intended applications: Ports and specialized machinery.

Reuse opportunity: Rail/wheel clamps, locks, turning/locking/braking integrations.

Make/buy: Partner first; design against signed system requirements.

Required evidence: Wind/rail interface, static holding, fault states and system integration.

Hazardous-area and personnel-transport uses need distinct qualification projects.

Competitor references: dellner-bubenzer, svendborg, twiflex, stromag. See the manufacturer collection reports for source URLs and gaps.
