## What changed in the company concept

The original marketing mockup asserted measured torque, simple interchangeability and a general temperature limit without evidence. The new website presents a research programme, shows maturity explicitly and links technical statements to sources. Monitoring is an existing competitor capability; differentiation remains a hypothesis to be tested.
