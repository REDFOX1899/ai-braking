# Mechanical concept release C0 — 7 September 2026

Created twelve original representative product concepts with CadQuery, downloadable STEP geometry, component identifiers, nominal review sheets, BOMs and explicit bought-component envelopes. Added interactive product workspaces with blue/navy/cyan styling, a selectable assembly navigator, exploded views and targets separated from verified evidence.

C0 is for engineering review only. No detailed mechanism, strength, thermal, hydraulic, electrical or physical qualification is claimed. The drum linkage, purchased actuators, flexible element, buffer internals and rail-clamp mechanism are not production designs. Production tolerances, GD&T, material condition, hardware retention, fastening preload and test acceptance require a responsible engineer’s approval.

Verification includes CAD solid validity, STEP volume round trips, static overlap reports, document rendering and website build checks. Static geometry checks do not validate moving clearance, contact, load paths or manufacturability. See the mechanical development plan for phases and required inputs.
