Decades of R&D investments and the extensive accumulated know-how of our employees make us experts in our field. Thus we can credibly assist you in your efforts to achieve the same status in your field, no matter how challenging your operation, and no matter how remote your location.
We are global because you are
Our global mindset and vision enable us to deliver our market-leading industrial hydraulic brake system and services across branches and borders alike. In other words, we are able to serve you and service your operation no matter when and where. Whether your projects are based in a remote area of the United States, a wind farm on the North Sea, the highlands of Peru or demanding regions with low to middle wind speeds. We are global because you are.
