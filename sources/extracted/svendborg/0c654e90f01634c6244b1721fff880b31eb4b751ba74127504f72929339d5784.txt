Brake Pads Green Solution
A GREEN ALTERNATIVE
Our inhouse developed green brake pads meet all the strict EU environmental regulations. We always strive to comply to all Automotive rules and directives including restriction of use of hazardous substances related to OE and AM applications.
Green Friction Material
In order to focus on environmental issues, friction materials for both SB1002 and SB1003 are developed according to the REACH legislation. In short, it means  they are Asbestos Free and contain:
Less than 0.1% Mercury
Less than 0.1% Chromium 6+
Less than 0.1% Lead
Less than 0.01% Cadmium
All electrical items comply with the RoHS directive.
