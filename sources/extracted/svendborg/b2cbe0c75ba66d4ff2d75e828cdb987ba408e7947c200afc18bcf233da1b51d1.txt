Yaw Disc Resurfacing
Disc Resurfacing
Developed in-house by Svendborg Brakes the Yaw Disc Resurfacing service powered by our engineered tools marks a breakthrough in disc and ring renovation efficiency. The disc/ring is milled uptower to remove damages to extend the lifetime of the disc with minimal downtime and investment. Our certified service engineers use their experience of hundereds of turbines to ensure consistent quality and performance in the field.

Minimize Downtime
Svendborg Brakes offers a specialized disc resurfacing service designed to restore yaw discs or yaw rings to optimal condition after years of wear and tear. The uptower refurbishment service minimizes turbine downtime and reduces maintenance expenses compared to replacing the worn or damaged part.
Key Service Features
On-site and uptower refurbishment of upper and lower disc/ring
Fast setup and execution, ensuring minimal disruption to turbine operations
Compact tool is easy to handle by our technicians and fits turbines with tight spaces
Extensive experience of machining brake discs and yaw rings uptower
Holistic optimization of brake and yaw sliding system backed by knowledge of a pioneer in wind brakes
Why Choose Our Service
Proven technology ensuring high-quality results
Avoids the logistical and financial burden of disc or ring replacement
Performed by expert technicians with deep knowledge of turbines
Backed by Regal Rexnord’s commitment to innovation and reliability
Specifications
Yaw System: Fits 90 & 120 style and sliding systems
Power supply: 400v 50Hz 16A
Max power consumption: 4Kw
Feed Rate:15 mm per second (normal yaw speed)
Crane capacity needed: 50 kg
Access to yaw section: 400 mm x 500 mm minimum
