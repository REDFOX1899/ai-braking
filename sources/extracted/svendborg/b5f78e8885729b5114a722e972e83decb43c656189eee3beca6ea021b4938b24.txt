ROADSHOW
EUROPEAN ROADSHOW
After an incredible reception in 2023 and 2024, we continued the roadshow through 2025 - and we we not stopping. We’re excited to hit the road again in
2026
- bringing our latest innovations and enhanced aftermarket expertise directly to customers across Europe.
These on-site visits offer a unique opportunity to experience our technology up close and firsthand.
Inside the roadshow truck, you'll discover customized live demonstrations featuring select products and maintenance technologies, plus the chance to discuss specific challenges - and gain insights into our outstanding service setup.
The roadshow primarily focuses on delivering advanced solutions for
Wind
and
Port Crane
applications. However, we're always open to exploring other areas where our technical capabilities and industry experience can provide added value.
And on a final note, while our Roadshow Tour currently centers on Europe, our comprehensive global aftermarket and service network ensures full support for clients worldwide.
