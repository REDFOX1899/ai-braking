Corporate Social Responsibility
Supporting the UN Sustainable Development Goals
Corporate Social Responsibility has for many years been an integrated part of running Svendborg Brakes. It is therefore a natural next step for Svendborg Brakes, to support the
United Nations Sustainable Development Goals
in a joint UN effort to better health, working conditions, working procedures and to take better care of the environment.
The 17 UN Goals were agreed by the world's heads of states at a UN meeting in 2015, aimed at creating a better world by 2030.
Our Areas of Focus
We have initially chosen to dedicate our attention to four of the UN goals and thereby strengthen some of our existing procedures even more:
World Goal no. 3 Good Health and Well Being
World Goal no. 5 Gender Equality
World Goal no. 8 Decent Work and Economic Growth
World Goal no. 12 Responsible Consumption and Production
About
The
Sustainable Development Goals
are 17 interlinked global goals and 169 sub-goals designed to be a ‘blueprint to achieve a better and more sustainable future for all’. The goals commit all 193 UN member states to eradicate poverty and hunger in the world, reduce inequalities, ensure good education, better health, decent jobs and a more sustainable economic growth.
The SDGs were set in New York on the 25th September 2015 by the United Nations General Assembly.
