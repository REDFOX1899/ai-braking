Oil & Gas
We provide superior braking solutions for the oil & gas industry
We have extensive experience in designing, supplying and servicing brakes for the tough environments and demanding conditions of the oil & gas industry.
This means that Svendborg Brakes can offer a full range of solutions for both on and off shore operations such as draw works, top drives, cranes and vessels.
Our solutions are designed for the high degree of reliability and safety, which is essential in these demanding environments. Products are comprehensively tested and carefully certified to meet the special demands of the oil & gas industry.
