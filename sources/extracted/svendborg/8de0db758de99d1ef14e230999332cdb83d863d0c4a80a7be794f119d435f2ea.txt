Mining
We take pride in being the trusted provider for the mining industry
Mining operations are among the most challenging industrial tasks. Locations are often remote, and conditions are harsh. At Svendborg Brakes we understand these challenges, and we strive to deliver safe and reliable solutions to help you overcome them.
The industry has transformed significantly over the years, with conveyor OEMs engineering larger and more complex systems. These advanced conveyors are built to provide greater strength and capacity, increasing the demand for more sophisticated braking solutions. Mine operators now need systems that are not only faster and smarter but also easier to use. In response to these industry changes, Svendborg Brakes introduced the SOBO iQ.
Below are some examples of highly engineered Svendborg Brakes products providing exceptional performance in a wide range of applications:
Ball & Sag Mills
Downhill Conveyors
Incline Conveyors
Overland Conveyors
Shovels & Draglines
Stackers & Reclaimers
Hoists / Winders
Car Tipplers
Car Positioners
Tensioning Winches
Take-up Winches
We take great pride in providing the best industrial hydraulic brake system for the industry offering everything from brakes and hydraulics to electronic and service contracts to meet the demands of the mining industry.
We provide braking solutions you can depend upon to perform, day in and day out. We know that your up time is crucial to your operation, and down time is costly and unacceptable. Thus our braking solutions are designed, tested and produced to cope with the extreme working conditions of the mining industry.
