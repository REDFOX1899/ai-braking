CURRENT PRODUCT PERFORMANCE
We connect digital/analog sensors to our “cloud” where data gets calculated and analyzed. It gives a “live-look” of the product's performance and the process efficiency. Easy to retrofit on existing systems within all customer segments and industries
Svendborg Brakes connectivity projects are installations at mine conveyor drive stations, bucket wheel excavators and crane applications. At these assignments, we monitor brake timings and brake torque to ensure they work exactly as intended. We also monitor valves and oil purity as well as we detect if accumulators, motor/pump systems are defect or damaged. Connectivity are all about custom-built solutions.
