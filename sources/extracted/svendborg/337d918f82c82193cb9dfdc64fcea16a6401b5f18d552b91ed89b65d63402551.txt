Service Tools
SPECIALIZED TOOLS
A selection of specialized tools are available to support maintenance, and diagnostics of wind turbine systems. While some tools can be purchased individually, others are in-house developed and used exclusively by certified service engineers as part of professional service delivery. This ensures access to standalone tools alongside high-quality, engineer-led services aligned with industry standards.
YAW DISC RESURFACING
A service solution that completely renovates worn yaw discs.
LIFTING & INSTALLATION TOOLS
Yaw brake service tools. Safe and fast way to remove and install yaw brakes.
GROOVE TOOL
The anti-glazing groove tool solution providing reduced yaw noise.
UCCASE
Smart technology for monitoring product performance & predictive maintenance.
PISTON EXTRACTOR
Safe & fast removal of pistons from yaw brake 90 & 120 series during service & repair.
