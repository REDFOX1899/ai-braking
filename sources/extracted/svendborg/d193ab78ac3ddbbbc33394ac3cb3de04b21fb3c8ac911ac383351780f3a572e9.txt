Green Friction Material
Introducing Svendborg Brakes Friction Material SB1002 + SB1003 For Yaw Application
In order to focus on environmental issues, friction materials for both SB1002 and SB1003 are developed according to the REACH legislation. In short, it means they are Asbestos Free and contain:
Less than 0.1% Mercury
Less than 0.1% Chromium 6+
Less than 0.1% Lead
Less than 0.01% Cadmium
All electrical items comply with the RoHS directive.
Advantages
Reduced weight
Visual wear indicator
Corrosion protected
Cut-off corners
Optional wire for electrical wear indication
Reduced debris build-up
Less glazing and reduced noise
In addition to the above, Svendborg Brakes always strive to comply to all Automotive rules and directives (Restriction of use of hazardous substances), related to OE and AM applications.
