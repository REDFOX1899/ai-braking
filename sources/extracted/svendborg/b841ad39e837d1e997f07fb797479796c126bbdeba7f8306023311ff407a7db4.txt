LBS 90/120
The Lifting & Installation Tools LBS 90/120 are for servicing or replacing of yaw brakes in all wind turbines using the 120 braking series. It is easy to manoeuvre making it much faster and safer for the service technicians to carry out their work. It is available in Designed and developed by Svendborg Brakes engineers.
