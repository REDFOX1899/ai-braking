Marine
We take pride in being the trusted provider for the marine industry
We have decades of experience in designing, supplying and servicing brakes for a range of marine applications. This means that Svendborg Brakes can offer a full range of ready-to-install products, ideal for offshore and ship-mounted winches, ship propellers, deck machinery, cranes etc. Our solutions are designed for the high degree of reliability and safety which is essential in these demanding environments. Stainless steel components and high tech coatings also provide a high grade of corrosion protection.
