Cooling Systems
OUR COOLING SOLUTIONS
Keeping nacelle at optimum temperature and delivering outstanding performance while minimizing complexity and maintenance. Passive cooling elements for installation on top of wind turbines. Isolating valves and air excluding manifolds.
KEY BENEFITS
Cooling capacity: 248 kW
Coolant medium: Water/glycol mixture
Coolant flow rate: 650 L/min
Design temperature: -20 to +50°C
Expected lifetime: 20 years
