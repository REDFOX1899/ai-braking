Piston Extractor
PISTON EXTRACTOR
Designed and developed by Svendborg Brakes engineers. Safe and fast removal of brake pistons from the yaw brake 90 and 120 series during service and repair.
How it works
Piston Extractor is placed on the BSAB 120 brake series. Handle rotation makes threaded spindle turn and pull out piston from brake yoke. Complete weight of the piston extractor tool is 6.5 kg.
Key Benefits
Easy Use
Improved Safety
Reduction in Seal and Piston Install/Removal Time By Up to 50%
Reduced Down Time & Costs
Reduced Technician Fatigue
Easy Transportation
