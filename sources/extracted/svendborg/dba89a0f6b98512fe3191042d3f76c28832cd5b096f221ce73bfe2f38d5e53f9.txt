Samples of Polymer
Polymer Friction Material For Yaw Slide Systems And Yaw Brake Applications
The Polymer friction material is developed and manufactured for all types of brakes and used widely for yaw sliding systems and yaw brake applications. The product family offers various friction qualities, tailored to fulfil customer needs for performance and environmental specifications, making it ideal for brake systems in Wind Turbines.
Key Benefits
Polymer friction material for static and dynamic braking
Stable friction performance, low sensitivity to speed and pressure
Good wear properties and temperature resistance for low maintenance cost
Extensive testing prior to release of any new material
Decades of know-how from in-house R&D and own full scale test centers
The ability to simulate individual applications
Option for supply with various monitoring systems
