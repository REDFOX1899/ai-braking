Disc Resurfacing Tools
SERVICE SOLUTION
Developed over several generations by Svendborg Brakes engineers, the yaw disc resurfacing tools mark a breakthrough in wind energy service tooling. The resurfacing tools
reflect our commitment to technical excellence and reliability.
These in-house developed tools are not sold separately, but used exclusively by certified service engineers as part of our professional support—ensuring consistent quality and performance in the field.

Minimize Downtime
Traditional replacement of discs is a time consuming and costly operation. The inhouse developed service tools completely renovates yaw discs after years of wear and tear and, as a result, minimizes service downtime and maintenance.
Why Choose Our Service
Patented and proven technology ensuring high-quality results
Avoids the logistical and financial burden of disc replacement
Performed by expert technicians with deep knowledge of turbines
Backed by Regal Rexnord’s commitment to innovation and reliability
Key Benefits
Refurbish damaged or worn yaw discs uptower
Fully in-house developed tool and software
Bolts directly onto turbine machine frame
Can machine both sides of disc if needed
Quick and short setup and machine time
Specifications
Yaw System: Fits 90 & 120 style and sliding systems
Power supply: 400v 50Hz 16A
Max power consumption: 4Kw
Feed Rate:15 mm per second (normal yaw speed)
Crane capacity needed: 50kg
Access to yaw section: 400mm x 500mm minimum
