Thruster Brakes
THRUSTER BRAKES
The electro-hydraulic thruster brake is a heavy-duty brake, designed to withstand the severe conditions of heavy-duty machinery in various industries. Energizing the electro-hydraulic thruster releases the brake shoes from the drum. When the electro-hydraulic thruster is de-energized, the brake is activated, as the levers apply the brake shoes to the drum.
