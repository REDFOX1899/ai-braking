Limit Switches
Geared Cam Limit Switches for Port Cranes are used in port cranes to provide precise control over crane movements, ensuring accurate stopping at predefined points for enhanced operational safety. They can be easily adjusted and customized to meet specific application requirements, allowing for flexibility in various crane operations
>
Synthetic housing and metal housing
>
Large cam disc diameter
>
2 - 8 contacts
>
Installation of incremental- and absolute encoders possible
>
Cam adjustments at fixed position in the housing with a gear ratio of 146
>
Modular design
>
Positive opening  according to IEC 60974-5-1 Annex K
>
Housing Material:synthetic
>
Degree of Protection IP: IP66
>
Nominal Revolution: 4.1 - 16,000
>
Electrical Rating Rating: AC-15 DC-13
>
Max Input Speed RPM:1000 - 1800
>
Design: Front/foot/mounting flange
