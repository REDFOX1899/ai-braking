SIBRE Siegerland Bremsen GmbH is a reliable partner for project developers, plant manufacturers, crane manufacturers, operators of complex industrial plants, heavy industry companies and manufacturers of wind turbines. We supply tailor-made industrial braking systems and high-quality drive components that are characterized by maximum operational safety, certified quality in accordance with ISO 9001 and an exceptionally long service life.
