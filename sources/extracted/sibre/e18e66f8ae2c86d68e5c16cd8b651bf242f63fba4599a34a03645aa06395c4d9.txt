High speed
Our flexible couplings provide a reliable, vibration-damping connection between the drive and the driven shaft—even under the most demanding conditions. They are used wherever angular, radial, and axial misalignment, as well as the reduction of shocks and vibrations, are required—whether in conveyor technology, mechanical engineering, or industrial drive systems.
