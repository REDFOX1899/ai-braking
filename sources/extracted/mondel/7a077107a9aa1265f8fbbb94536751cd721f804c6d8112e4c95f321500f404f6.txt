Crane Brake Products
Overhead crane brakes are the one component that can continue holding a load if all other equipment fails on a crane. Magnetek® brakes are available in a wide range of designs, including industrial duty, heavy duty, mill duty, drop-in, vertical, and motor mounted, and are ideal for harsh environments and applications including heavy moveable structures, lock gates, bridges and more.
