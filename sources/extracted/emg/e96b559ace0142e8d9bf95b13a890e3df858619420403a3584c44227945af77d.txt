Operating principle
The electrical connection of the units must be made in parallel with the motors. Electrical connection of the ELDRO
®
motors in series is not possible.
The ELDRO
®
DC series is also used for opening brakes in the event of a power failure of the three-phase supply. For this purpose, the units are installed in the brake as an additional thruster and can then be supplied with a DC voltage, for example from a battery. This can then be used, for example, to safely set down loads in the hoist.
