Function principle
The hydraulic control unit HR and the hydraulic power unit HA reliably generate hydraulic energy for any power range. A hydraulic control unit HR is a hydraulic power unit HA including servo valve SV that controls the volume flow of the fluid.
For strip guiding systems, hydraulic power units with a rating of between 0.55 and 18.5 kW are normally used.
Accessories for Standard HR/HA (optional):
Standby motor/pump assembly
Oil tank and piping in stainless steel
Heater
If cooling is required, either air or water cooling is available.
