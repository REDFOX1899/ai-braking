Twiflex is a global leader in innovative braking solutions designed to provide years of reliable performance.

Twiflex

Twiflex Limited, founded in 1946, specializes in the design, manufacture and supply of advanced braking technologies for industrial applications. Twiflex offers the largest range of pneumatically-, hydraulically-, and mechanically-applied industrial disc brake calipers in the world, along with a full line of thrusters, disc brake systems, flexible couplings and shafts, and clutch couplings.
