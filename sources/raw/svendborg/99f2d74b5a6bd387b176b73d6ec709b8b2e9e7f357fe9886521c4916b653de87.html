About

Svendborg Brakes is a global leader in intelligent hydraulic braking solutions and pioneers within hydraulic braking solutions for industries like wind, mining, container handling, marine, and oil & gas. Their innovations reduce maintenance and downtime, delivering the performance OEMs expect. With worldwide facilities, they offer top-tier aftermarket service using OEM-specified parts and specialized service tools.
