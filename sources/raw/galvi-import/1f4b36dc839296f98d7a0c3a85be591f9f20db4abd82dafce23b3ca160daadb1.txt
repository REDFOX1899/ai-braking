Failsafe Shoe Brakes as per DIN 15435     standard, with electrohydraulic a.c. HYDRO     GALVI Thrusters with internal not adjustable     springs and without external adjustable main   spring set.
STANDARD FEATURES
• HYDRO GALVI a.c. electrohydraulic Thruster (IP.65, class F) (HYD)
• Internal springs for HYDRO GALVI Thruster (IS)
• Aluminium DIN 15435 brake Shoes with steel bushings (CD)
• Asbestos free linings with theoretical friction coefficient μ =  0,42 (42)
• Galvanized pins (PS)
• Self lubricating bushes on main pins (AU)
• Galvanized pins, tie rods, and small items (ZN)
• Epoxy antisalt paint final colour GREY RAL 7001
• Cast iron levers and base
AVAILABLE OPTIONAL  ITEMS
• Self adjusting device (NV)
• Extra-wide brake Shoes (1,5 times wider than the DIN standard  brake Shoes) (CL)
• Stainless steel pins (SS)
• Lubricators on main pins (GR)
• Open position mechanical microswitch fitted on Thruster (SWMU)
• Linings’ wear mechanical microswitch     on left (L) or right (R) side (FIC-L/R)
• Supporting spheres for vertical brake axis     on opposite side to FIC optional items’s side
(not available for models with Thrusters 024, 031, 051 and 081)     (it is necessary to have Thruster with foot rotated 90 degrees)  (ORZ-L/R)
• Lowering valve for HYDRO Thruster
(not available for Thrusters 024, 031, 051 and 081) (LO)
• Lifting valve for HYDRO Thruster
(not available for Thrusters 024, 031, 051 and 081) (LI)
