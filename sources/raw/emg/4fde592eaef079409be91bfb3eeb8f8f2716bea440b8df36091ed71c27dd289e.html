EMG ELDRO® New Generation

We have perfected the proven technology of EMG thrusters in a new generation: EMG ELDRO® - THE Thruster.
  • Contact us directly
  • Brochure

EMG ELDRO® THE thruster - 100% customized, 100% quality, 100% durability and 100% reliability

Function principle

Every day, our customers entrust us with the safety of their equipment and goods, but more importantly, they entrust us with the safety of the people who work with crane systems and suspended loads. 

Our EMG product portfolio serves every conceivable customer requirement, regardless of industry, environmental conditions, installation size and location, or force or speed requirement.  We deliver 100% variety, 100% quality, 100% durability and 100% reliability.

Based on flow numerical calculations and simulations - know-how we now have in-house - we have perfected the proven technology of EMG thrusters in a new generation: EMG ELDRO® - THE Thruster.

The new generation EMG ELDRO® combines the best of our previous ELDRO® and ELHY® series, incorporates all the experience gained over decades for applications in various areas. 

At the same time, we remain true to the purely mechanical functional principle, which does not include any electric valves or electronic components in the basic functionality. 

As always, we thus ensure 100 % reliability and maximum ease of maintenance.

THE ORIGINAL. BE SAFE.

Customer benefits
  • More safety and comfort & higher performance with smaller sizes
  • Higher efficiency and faster cycle times & application in higher temperature ranges
  • Inner position displays & higher ease of maintenance

 

Perfect for these industries

Ports (Container / Bulk material)

EMG thrusters can be found on crane systems and container gantry cranes in all ports worldwide.

Steel & Metallurgy

Optimum guiding and quality assurance in the steel manufacturing industry with EMG solutions.

Raw material extraction / handling

When it comes to extracting raw materials, plant operators worldwide trust our quality products for heavy duty logistics.

More thruster solutions

  • EMG ELHY®

    Electrohydraulic thruster: EMG offers proven, robust lifting equipment for particularly demanding applications, including steelworks and mining. 

  • EMG ELDROclassic®

    EMG ELDRO® is an electrohydraulic thruster system with 100% failsafe on its safety component. Fully tested in function and endurance.

  • EMG ELDRO® Ex series

    Explosion-proof units EMG ELDRO® for chemical/petrochemical industry, pharmaceuticals, oil/gas production, mining, food industry, biofuels, waste water.

  • EMG ELDRO® Direct current

    EMG ELDRO® DC units for systems with existing DC mains and also ideal for opening brakes in the event of a power failure of the three-phase power supply.

The ELDRO® thruster has existed as a registered trademark since 1931 and has therefore been successful on the market for more than 90 years - an insanely successful product!

 

EMG ELDRO® meets all requirements of our customers, increases the safety of their brakes and is 100% tested.

 

With this pretention EMG sets the industry standard for electrohydraulic brake thrusters.
