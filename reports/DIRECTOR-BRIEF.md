# AI Braking — technical partnership meeting

## The proposition

Agree one supplier-referenced pilot: GALVI N.315.HYD.030/05 brake and PD.315 drum are the initial catalogue references. We bring original integration concepts, a traceable dimension register, an interactive CAD workspace and an explicit release plan. No partnership or supplier endorsement is established.

## What can truthfully be said

“We have a concept and integration package ready for technical review. We want to agree current interfaces, detailed engineering responsibilities and qualification gates before manufacturing release.”

Do not describe C0 as manufacturing-ready. Do not transfer GALVI speed, torque or material ratings to our simplified geometry. Do not call a reserved solid envelope a designed brake mechanism.

## Source basis

GALVI Shoe Brakes catalogue, February 2016. PDF page 4 (printed 4–5) identifies the selected brake. PDF page 15 (printed 26–27) identifies PD.315. The source hash is recorded in the downloadable dimension register. Current supplier confirmation remains outstanding.

Important interpretation: H=220 mm is shown twice, from the drum axis to each mounting column. Full longitudinal pitch is 2H=440 mm; transverse pitch I=80 mm; four holes M=18 mm. Do not use 220 mm as the full longitudinal pitch.

## Proposed own geometry

Adapter 762 x 220 x 20 mm; 50 mm shaft bore selection; 20 mm radial drum wall and 15 mm flat web; proposed M16 hardware; 200 mm display envelope width. These are assumptions, not GALVI manufacturing dimensions. Coupling holes in the PD.315 reference are not mounting-bolt holes.

## Ask the director

1. Confirm the appropriate current brake, drum, thruster and options for one specified customer duty.
2. Supply controlled drawings, vendor STEP, tolerances, installation and fastener instructions, material/lining specification and force-stroke-time data.
3. Allocate responsibility for selection, integration, independent checking, supply quality, test execution and release approval.
4. Agree one pilot and laboratory quotation before tooling or production commitments.

## Release gates

Prepared: reference selection, transcribed public dimensions and original nominal interface model.
Open: current supplier confirmation, machine duty, shaft connection, full mechanism, material condition, tolerances, joint calculations, structural/thermal work, physical tests, inspection criteria and responsible engineering sign-off.

Success is an agreed technical review and pilot scope. No unsupported product rating, competitor improvement multiple or manufacturing-readiness claim is required to make the proposition credible.
